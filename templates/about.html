About Lumen

Helping readers find the right book, not just another book.

Lumen is an AI-powered book discovery prototype built to make a large catalogue feel personal. It combines reader behaviour, book metadata and rating signals so users can discover popular titles, explore similar books and build recommendations around their own taste.

Our purpose

Make discovery feel human.

Instead of asking readers to understand algorithms, Lumen uses the books readers love and rate highly as positive taste signals. Search and save features support discovery and reading-list management, while the AI methods stay measurable and explainable for evaluation.

How it works

Three recommendation approaches, one reading experience

Lumen implements multiple recommender approaches for technical comparison, while keeping the reader-facing experience simple and automatic.

01
Content-Based Filtering

Similar book profile

Compares title, author, publisher and publication period using TF-IDF and cosine similarity. This is especially useful when a book has limited reader-rating history.

02
Collaborative Filtering

Similar reader behaviour

Uses item-based collaborative filtering to find books that receive similar explicit rating patterns from active readers in the dataset.

03
Hybrid Filtering

Balanced recommendations

Combines collaborative and content evidence for personalised and related-book recommendations, with automatic fallback when one source of evidence is sparse.

Built for responsible discovery

What Lumen is designed to do

This prototype focuses on transparent recommendation logic, useful reader controls and a clear separation between recommendation methods. Saved books, loved books and personal ratings are stored locally for the demo reading profile.

01

Discover. Search a large catalogue by title, author, publisher or ISBN.

02

Personalise. Loved books and personal ratings of 8–10/10 create positive taste signals for the For You shelf; lower ratings remain stored feedback but are not treated as positive seeds.

03

Stay consistent. Dataset reader ratings and personal ratings both use the same visible 1–10 scale, keeping the data and interface consistent.

04

Evaluate. Collaborative, content-based and hybrid approaches can be compared using accuracy and efficiency metrics.
